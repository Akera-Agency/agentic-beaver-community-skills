# Copywriting Frameworks

Quick reference for email and copy structures.

---

## AICDC (Attention-Interest-Conviction-Desire-Close)

1. **Attention:** Draw in the audience with a bold statement or provocative question
2. **Interest:** Pique their interest with an intriguing fact or claim
3. **Conviction:** Provide solid proof that your product/service delivers
4. **Desire:** Paint a vivid picture of the benefits they'll get
5. **Close:** Prompt them to act with a compelling CTA

---

## HSO (Hook-Story-Offer)

1. **Hook:** Grab attention with a provocative statement or question
2. **Story:** Share a story that connects with their problem or desire
3. **Offer:** Present your product/service as the solution

---

## PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution)

1. **Problem:** Identify a problem your audience faces
2. **Agitate:** Stir up emotions by emphasizing the pain
3. **Solution:** Present your offering as the answer

---

## FAS (Fear-Agitation-Solution)

1. **Fear:** Identify a genuine fear your audience has
2. **Agitation:** Delve into potential consequences of that fear
3. **Solution:** Present your product/service as the solution to alleviate it

---

## SCR (Situation-Complication-Resolution)

1. **Situation:** Outline a situation your target audience finds themselves in
2. **Complication:** Introduce a challenge that arises
3. **Resolution:** Show how your product/service resolves it

---

## PPP (Promise-Proof-Proposal)

1. **Promise:** Promise a significant benefit or transformation
2. **Proof:** Show how your product/service has delivered before
3. **Proposal:** Propose a next step (trial, consultation, purchase)

---

## ELA (Empathy-Logic-Action)

1. **Empathy:** Show you understand their problem
2. **Logic:** Explain logically how your product solves it
3. **Action:** Urge them to take action

---

## GLF (Gain-Logic-Fear)

1. **Gain:** Show what they stand to gain
2. **Logic:** Use logical arguments for why you're the best choice
3. **Fear:** Highlight what they lose if they don't act

---

## HEA (Highlight-Explain-Action)

1. **Highlight:** Highlight a unique feature or benefit
2. **Explain:** Explain how it works and why it's useful
3. **Action:** Encourage the next step

---

## Hero's Journey

1. **Call to Adventure:** Present the problem they face
2. **Meet a Guide:** Introduce your brand as the guide
3. **The Plan:** Show your solution
4. **Call to Action:** Encourage them to act and enjoy the transformation

---

## IBPA (Intrigue-Benefit-Proof-Action)

1. **Intrigue:** Start with a fascinating fact or question
2. **Benefit:** Highlight the primary benefit you provide
3. **Proof:** Back up claims with testimonials, case studies, or stats
4. **Action:** Clear and compelling CTA

---

## SPEA (Snapshot-Promise-Expand-Action)

1. **Snapshot:** Quick snapshot of their current problem or desire
2. **Promise:** Promise a significant benefit
3. **Expand:** Delve deeper into how you deliver
4. **Action:** Encourage the next step

---

## TPS (Tease-Please-Seize)

1. **Tease:** Tantalizing hint at the value you offer
2. **Please:** Deliver valuable content or insights
3. **Seize:** Encourage them to seize the opportunity

---

## AFCR (Authority-Familiarity-Credibility-Reciprocity)

1. **Authority:** Establish your authority in the field
2. **Familiarity:** Connect on a shared problem or desire
3. **Credibility:** Prove credibility with evidence of past success
4. **Reciprocity:** Offer something of value to foster goodwill

---

## SOS (Situation-Opportunity-Solution)

1. **Situation:** Describe a situation they frequently encounter
2. **Opportunity:** Introduce an opportunity that arises
3. **Solution:** Explain how you help seize that opportunity
