# Cold Email Examples

## Campaign Context

**Target:** Skool community owners (course creators, coaches)
**Offer:** AI agents + n8n workflows to improve retention/LTV
**Company:** Akera

---

## Email 1: First Touch

### Original (Version A) — 131 words ❌
```
Hey {{firstName}},

Reached out because I noticed your Skool program might be facing what we've seen with a lot of creators. Only 10–20% of members finish the course. That early drop-off crushes LTV.

When people stop engaging in week two, they rarely upsell or refer friends, no matter how good the content is.

We helped a few other Skool creators tackle this. In your case, you can use n8n workflows to automate student engagement. That kind of outcome early = way higher completion, way better retention, and $50K–$300K recovered in LTV.

Have you tried using AI agents in one of your programs yet?
```

**Issues:**
- 131 words (way over 75)
- Generic opener "Reached out because I noticed..."
- Vague numbers "$50K-$300K"
- Weak CTA "Have you tried..."

### Improved — 62 words ✅
```
{{firstName}} —

{{memberCount}} members in {{communityName}}. What's your completion rate?

Industry average: 10-20% finish. The rest ghost by week 2.

We fixed this for [Creator X] with AI workflows. Result: ${{specificAmount}} recovered in LTV.

Takes 10 min to see if it fits yours.

Worth a quick look?
```

**Why it works:**
- 62 words (under 75)
- Opens with their specific data
- Specific social proof placeholder
- Direct CTA

```
quick update {{firstName}},

After watching more of your content (really impressive, by the way), I had my team dig deeper into how a platform with AI agents could support your students inside the course, 24/7.

👉 [Watch Loom]

```

**Issues:**
- Too long
- Compliments feel generic
- "I had my team dig deeper" — no one cares
- PS feels disconnected

### Improved — 71 words ✅
```
{{firstName}} —

Watched some of {{communityName}}. Your teaching style's legit.

But even great content loses people to life stuff. Jobs, kids, Netflix.

What if something inside the course kept them accountable 24/7?

Did this for a coaching program: churn dropped 27% → 15%. Saved them $412K.

Made you a quick Loom showing how it'd work for yours:

👉 [Watch Loom]
```

**Why it works:**
- Specific compliment (teaching style)
- Relatable problem (life stuff)
- Concrete numbers
- Loom feels like effort, not spam

## Email 5: Hail Mary

### Original — 95 words ❌
```
Hey there {{firstName}},

Totally cool if this isn't the right time.

But I didn't want to ghost this thread without making one last offer.

If you're even a little curious about what AI agents could unlock for {{cleanedCommunityName}}, I'd be happy to build an AI coach inside your Skool group {{keyOutcome}} by automating the exact steps you teach.

No pitch. I'll build it for free to show what's possible on the next level.

If it doesn't make money, discard it. If it does, like it has for multiple of our clients this year, we can discuss scaling.
```

### Improved — 48 words ✅
```
{{firstName}} —

Totally fine if timing's off.

Last offer: I'll build you a free AI coach for {{communityName}}. No strings.

Works? We talk.
Doesn't? Delete it.

Reply "show me" and I'll send the plan.

Either way, good luck with the community 🤝
```

**Why it works:**
- 48 words (way under limit)
- Zero pressure
- Binary CTA ("show me")
- Ends warm
