## Key Takeaways

1. **Follow-up with social proof** - Mention concrete results from other clients
2. **Compliment their work** - Shows you did research
3. **Handle objections with detail** - When they ask "what do you do", explain thoroughly
4. **Multiple case studies** - Different scales/types to show range
5. **Performance-based pricing** - Reduces risk for prospect
6. **Gentle persistence** - "Don't want to be too persistent but..."
7. **Always include Calendly** - Make booking frictionless
