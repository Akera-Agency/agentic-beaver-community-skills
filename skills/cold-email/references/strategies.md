# Cold Email Strategies

## Strategy 11 - Add A Guarantee

The biggest objection to overcome with cold prospects is trust. Guarantees help people trust you more because you have some skin in the game.

### Guarantee Types

**Deliverability based (best for services)**
- If we don't do X we'll do X
- If we don't increase your revenue in 60 days you'll get a full refund
- If we don't increase your revenue in 60 days we'll work for no extra charge until we get there

**Asset based (for audits/setup)**
- We guarantee that you'll have a new website in 30 days or you get a full refund
- We guarantee we'll set up your Facebook Ads with at least 10 ads in 30 days or you get a full refund

**Action-Based (best for courses and DIY offers)**
- If you follow all the steps in the action plan/course and you don't see (result) in (timeframe) you get a full refund

### Example
> Hey Tom
> We help SaaS companies like you get extra 10 meetings weekly with your ideal customers.
> And If we don't get you 10+ meetings weekly you get your money back.
> Mind if I send more info how we do it?

## Strategy 12 - Performance Based Offer

If your offer is performance based, say it in your emails.

> Hey Tom
> We help fashion ecom stores like yours grow with TikTok ads and we work purely on performance. If we don't increase your sales you don't pay us anything.
> Mind if I send more info?

---

## Strategy 13 - Free Audit

Offer your prospects a free audit based on your offer.

> Hey Tom
> We help fashion ecom stores like yours increase their traffic from Google. I already noticed some low-hanging fruit on {{companyName}} website and would love to give you a free audit on what you could improve.
> Do you have any time this Thursday or Friday for a 15 min call?

---

## Strategy 14 - Prework (Sending Value)

If you have a very specific offer and not many leads in your target group, do more prep:
- Do mockups
- Do quick audits
- Write down specific things you noticed on their website

---

## Strategy 15 - Broken Link Trick

If you don't want to create a customized video for every prospect and only create videos for people who respond:
- Send: "I created a quick loom video here: loom.com/fdfsjfhjhejf" (random string)
- When they click - it doesn't work. They reach out to tell you the link is broken
- Once they do - record a video and send the correct link

---

## Strategy 16 - Be Ambiguous

Sometimes being vague is better than being specific.

If your offer is something people have heard 100 times, they might be allergic to it even when you can help them. People may be biased from bad experiences.

**Example:** If you help SaaS companies with SEO, don't say "SEO" (they've heard it 1000 times). Say you have strategies to increase their MRR.

## Strategy 18 - Spontaneous

Most cold email copy sucks because you can tell straight away it's a template sent to 1000s of people.

**The fix:** Imagine you just found the prospect's website, clicked "email us" and are writing right then and there.

- Don't over-edit — editing removes authenticity
- Use words that "expert copywriters" would tell you to remove (like "so")
- Use "btw", "lmk" and casual text-speak
- Keep the signature simple

### Example
> Hey Tim
> So I was just searching for sustainable ecom stores and found {{companyName}}. And when I was scrolling through your product pages I saw that you don't have a TikTok account yet I wanted to reach out straight away.
> Have you thought about TikTok or?
> Because shortly we help sustainable ecom stores spread their mission and get more sales on TikTok.
> Btw we just helped (client) get an extra 40K visitors in 1 month there.
> Would it be cool if I send you more info on how we did it?
> Lmk.
> Best
> Alex
